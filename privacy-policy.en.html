Zikirmatik — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026 · Türkçe

This policy explains how the Zikirmatik mobile app (“the App”) handles your personal data. The App can be used fully offline and without an account; the processing described below applies only if you choose to create an optional account.

1. Data we process

  • Account details (optional): your email address, display name and (if you sign up with email) your password. Passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider; we never see them.
  • Usage and progress data: your dhikr counts, daily totals, surah memorization progress and app settings — for cloud backup and syncing between your devices.
  • Sign in with Google or Apple (optional): only your email and basic profile information are received, to verify your identity.
  • Advertising ID: the App shows Google AdMob ads (banner and full-screen). AdMob may use your device’s advertising identifier to serve and measure ads. On iOS you are asked for permission first; you can limit ad personalization in your device settings at any time.
  • Location (optional): if you allow it, your approximate location is used to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction for where you are. Location is only read while the App is open and is stored only on your device; it is never sent to our servers or synced with your account. The name of your city is looked up by your phone’s own operating system (Apple or Google) location service. If you do not allow it, times are calculated for the city you choose.
  • Photos: if you save a greeting card you made, the App asks only for permission to add it to your photos; it cannot see your photos.
  • Sharing: when you send a card to WhatsApp or another app, the content goes straight to that app; we do not see or store it.
  • Notifications: adhan, reminder, Jumu'ah and holy-day notifications are scheduled on your device; our server does not send you notifications and collects no push tokens.

The App does not record with the microphone or the camera and contains no analytics or tracking tools of its own.

2. Purpose and legal basis

Data is processed only to manage your account, back up your progress and sync it between your devices (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) — performance of a contract — and your consent; for users in Türkiye, KVKK Art. 5). The advertising identifier is processed to show ads.

3. Storage and transfers

Cloud data is stored with our infrastructure provider Supabase (EU — Frankfurt region). Each user’s data is isolated with row-level security (RLS); you can access only your own data. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing.

From time to time the App reads general settings, such as how often ads may appear, from our server; no personal data is sent when it does.

4. Offline and on-device data

Without an account, all your data stays only on your device and is never sent to a server. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

5. Your rights (GDPR / KVKK)

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data. You can delete your account and all of your server data directly in the App: Settings → Account → Delete account. Deletion is permanent and takes effect immediately. You can also use our account deletion page or write to us.
