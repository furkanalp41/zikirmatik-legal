Delete your account and data

Türkçe

In the Zikirmatik app you can permanently delete your account and all the data that goes with it (dhikr history, memorization progress, settings). This cannot be undone.

In the app (recommended)

  1. Open Zikirmatik.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and all of your data on our server are deleted immediately and permanently.

By email

If you can no longer use the app, email furkanalpgunay41@gmail.com from the address registered to your account, with the subject “Account deletion request”. We act on requests within 30 days.

What is deleted?

Your account, email address, display name, your whole dhikr history, daily totals, surah memorization progress and synced settings are deleted permanently. No backup of this data is kept after deletion.

For details, see the Privacy Policy.
